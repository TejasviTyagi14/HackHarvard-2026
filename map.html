      Mapping settings

      One camera uses Metric3D, a depth model that runs on your Mac's GPU (150 MB download the first time). No mounting or calibration needed; distances are within roughly 10%.

      m

      The one-camera depth model holds up to about 10 m indoors. With two cameras 12 cm apart, depth gets noisy past about 5 m.

      The compass follows how the whole image shifts, so it keeps turning with you even when there are few points to track. Distance walked always comes from the points.

      Map file

      The .ply opens in MeshLab, Blender or CloudCompare. A saved map keeps every keyframe, so it can be rebuilt; when you load one, start from where that mapping began, facing the same way.
