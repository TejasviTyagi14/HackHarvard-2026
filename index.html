          Calibrate with a person

          Works out the angle between the cameras, and with a second distance the spacing too, from someone standing in front of them. No clicking. You can also just say “calibrate”.

          1. Have someone stand straight ahead, facing the cameras, about 2 m away. Measure along the floor to where they stand.
            m
          2. Optional, to also solve the spacing: have them step back to a second measured distance, about twice as far.
            m

          Why two distances for the spacing: at a single distance, a wider spacing and a bigger angle look exactly the same, so one capture can only pin down one of them. It keeps your measured spacing and solves the angle, which is the hard one to measure.

            Camera check

            Point the rig at a still scene and keep everything still. If a camera keeps refocusing, distances will jitter.

            Vertical offset between cameras 0.0 px

            Learned from confident matches; a few pixels is normal. Over ~10 px means one camera is tilted.

            Detection & display

            Matching runs at 640 px wide either way. Drop to 640 × 480 if the second camera won't start (USB bandwidth).
